01 Component Trust Boundaries

Every external service is treated as untrusted unless the protocol explicitly grants authority.

ZERO-TRUST MODEL
System Layer Trust Status Operational Authority Scope
Web Frontend UNTRUSTED UI rendering only. Zero financial authorization authority.

02 Emergency Controls & Safety Invariants

Protocol-level controls designed to constrain execution during abnormal conditions.

Emergency Pause Mechanism

Automated Circuit Breaker

Consecutive execution failures can trigger the configured protection mechanism, preventing repeated external execution attempts during abnormal conditions.

Custody Boundary

Non-Custodial Architecture

Frontend, backend and advisory services do not become the source of truth for treasury ownership. Settlement authority remains constrained by the deployed protocol.

⛨
Authority Boundary

Advisory / Execution Separation

AI-generated policy assistance is separated from financial execution. The advisory layer does not hold execution signing authority.
