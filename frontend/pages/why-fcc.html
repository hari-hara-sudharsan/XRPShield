Why Flare Confidential Compute (FCC)?

Why public smart contracts alone cannot evaluate sensitive treasury risk parameters without exposing strategies

❌ WITHOUT FCC (Standard EVM Contract / Server)

Standard public smart contracts require parameter inputs in plaintext calldata. Any observer or front-running bot can inspect mempool transactions to deduce:

  • Exact drop trigger thresholds (e.g. -15% drop)
  • Target hedge allocation ratios
  • Maximum notional limits & stop loss positions

Alternatively, relying on a centralized off-chain server creates an operator trust bottleneck where the server operator can see, leak, or tamper with private strategies.

  • 🔒 Policy encrypted on client before submission
  • 🛡️ Decrypted ONLY inside TEE hardware RAM (SGX/SEV)
  • ⚡ Deterministic risk evaluation against live FTSOv2 price
  • 📜 Cryptographically signed EIP-712 ActionResult returned

The public blockchain verifies the attestation signature and executes the hedge WITHOUT ever learning the underlying risk parameters!
